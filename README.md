# pt-house-prices

[![License: CC BY 4.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-CC%20BY%204.0-lightgrey.svg)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

Interactive dashboard (`dist/index.html`) and publication-ready charts (`dist/charts/`) analysing housing price determinants across the **35 municipalities of the Lisbon (AML, 18) and Porto (AMP, 17) Metropolitan Areas** over **2009–2026** (constant 2026 €/m², 100% official empirical data).

## Contents

- `dist/index.html` — Self-contained interactive dashboard (zero external runtime requests).
- `dist/charts/` — Static charts (`.svg`, `.png`) and lightweight `.html` embeds.

## Third-Party Official Data Sources

Underlying statistical series remain subject to the respective terms and attribution requirements of their official providers:

- **Instituto Nacional de Estatística (INE)** — Bank appraisals, dwelling sales prices, construction supply, local rents, IRS income, demographics, and implicit mortgage interest rates ([CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)).
- **Turismo de Portugal (RNAL / SIGTUR)** — National Registry of Local Accommodation establishments ([CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)).
- **Banco de Portugal (BPstat) & European Central Bank / Eurosystem** — Euribor (3M/6M/12M), new housing credit volumes, and harmonised inflation (HICP).
- **Pordata (Fundação Francisco Manuel dos Santos)** — Municipal socioeconomic indicators and *Quadros Resumo*.
